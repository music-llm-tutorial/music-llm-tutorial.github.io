Applications for Composers

Applications for composers¶

Potential workflows include collaborative songwriting, composition support, and conversational post-production. The LLM may generate symbolic material directly or translate intent into calls to specialist music tools.

Design questions¶

  • What should remain under direct human control?

  • How can a producer inspect and undo each model action?

  • How should authorship, attribution, and training-data provenance be communicated?
