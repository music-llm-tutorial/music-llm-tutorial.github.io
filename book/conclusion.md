# Conclusion

The tutorial connects LLM mechanisms to three music-facing roles: listener,
producer, and performer. The goal is not merely to enumerate applications, but
to understand which capability enables each workflow and how to verify it.

## Open questions

- How can models demonstrate that an answer is grounded in musical evidence?
- Which evaluation protocols predict usefulness in real creative workflows?
- How should systems balance general language ability with specialist music tools?
- What interaction patterns preserve human agency and musical intent?
