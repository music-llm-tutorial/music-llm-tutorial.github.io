# LLMs for performers

Performers occupy a distinct position in the music AI landscape. Unlike listeners, who engage with music episodically, or producers, who work in single creative sessions, performers maintain a long-term relationship with their instrument, their repertoire, and the physical act of playing. This chapter follows that relationship through three layers: Machine perception of performance data (preliminaries for performance MIR), turning evidence into feedback and assessment (bridging performance and language), and sustaining coaching across time with agentic systems.
