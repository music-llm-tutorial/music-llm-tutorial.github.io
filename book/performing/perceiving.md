# Perceiving performance

A performance is a multi-layered event. It combines the written score — a symbolic specification of pitches, rhythms, and expressive markings — with an audio or video recording that captures how a specific musician realised those instructions at a specific moment. Bridging these two representations is a precondition for any meaningful automated analysis.
