From sequence models to foundation models¶

An autoregressive model represents a sequence by factorizing its probability:

p(x1:T)=∏t=1Tp(xt∣x<t).p(x_{1:T}) = \prod_{t=1}^{T} p(x_t \mid x_{<t}).p(x1:T​)=t=1∏T​p(xt​∣x<t​).
(1)

Scaling this objective across broad data and tasks yields a reusable model whose behavior can be adapted through context, instructions, or additional training.
