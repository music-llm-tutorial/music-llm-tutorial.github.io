LLMs × Music

Scalable Language Modeling Unlocks Music Applications¶

Welcome to the online companion for our ISMIR tutorial on the properties that make large language models useful for music understanding, recommendation, co-creation, and education.

Tutorial at a glance¶

  • Chapter 0 — Introduction (5 min): motivation and tutorial roadmap

  • Chapter 1 — Large Language Models (30 min): modeling, training, emergent capabilities, and evaluation

  • Chapter 2 — Music Understanding (30 min): audio, score, symbolic, and metadata-based music-language models

  • Chapter 3 — LLMs for Listeners: annotation, conversational retrieval, and recommendation

  • Chapter 4 — LLMs for Composers: songwriting, composition, and post-production

  • Chapter 5 — LLMs for Educators: performance assessment, coaching, and music education

  • Chapter 6 — Conclusion: recap, open research questions, and discussion

  • Appendix: shared resources and environment information

Presenters¶

Zachary Novack (Spotify), Seungheon Doh (Sony Group Corporation), Huan Zhang (Clefer), Yewon Kim (Carnegie Mellon University), and Keunwoo Choi (Upstage).
