# Pre-training, mid-training, and post-training

| Stage | Typical goal | Music-oriented example |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-training | Learn broad sequence regularities | Joint exposure to language and symbolic music |
| Mid-training | Add domain knowledge or capabilities | Continue training on music descriptions and notation |
| Post-training | Align behavior with instructions and preferences | Prefer useful, grounded responses to music questions |
