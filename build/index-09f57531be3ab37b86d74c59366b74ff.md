# LLMs × Music

## Scalable Language Modeling Unlocks Music Applications

Welcome to the online companion for our ISMIR tutorial on the properties that
make large language models useful for music understanding, recommendation,
co-creation, and education.

This book is a collaborative work in progress. Each chapter can be written in
[MyST Markdown](https://mystmd.org/guide) (`.md`) or as a Jupyter Notebook
(`.ipynb`).

## Tutorial at a glance

- **Chapter 0 — Introduction (5 min):** motivation and tutorial roadmap
- **Chapter 1 — Large Language Models (30 min):** modeling, training, emergent capabilities, and evaluation
- **Chapter 2 — Music Understanding (30 min):** audio, score, symbolic, and metadata-based music-language models
- **Chapter 3 — Musical Applications (120 min):** tools for listeners, producers, performers, and educators
- **Chapter 4 — Conclusion:** recap, open research questions, and discussion
