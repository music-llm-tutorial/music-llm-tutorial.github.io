# Applications for educators

<p style="color: #6b7280; font-size: 0.9rem;">Presenter: Huan Zhang</p>

Multimodal coaching systems may combine performance audio, video, notation, and
natural-language questions. Useful feedback must be specific, evidence-based,
appropriate to the learner, and actionable on the next attempt.

**Exercise:** Compare generic feedback (“improve expression”) with grounded
